ENTERPRISE PROCUREMENT POLICY
Acme Corporation — Version 3.2 — January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes procurement guidelines for all Acme Corporation employees.
It ensures consistent, transparent, and cost-effective purchasing across all departments.

2. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
All purchase orders must follow these approval requirements:
- Up to 5,000 USD: Department manager approval required.
- 5,001 to 25,000 USD: Senior manager and Finance sign-off required.
- 25,001 to 100,000 USD: VP-level approval and Legal review required.
- Above 100,000 USD: CFO and CEO approval mandatory. Board notification required.

3. VENDOR SELECTION
The Procurement team is responsible for vendor selection and onboarding.
All new vendors must complete a due diligence assessment before engagement.
Preferred vendors are listed in the Approved Vendor Registry maintained by Finance.
Employees must not engage vendors outside the Approved Vendor Registry without prior written approval from the Chief Procurement Officer.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
Standard payment terms are Net-30 from invoice date.
Early payment discounts must be approved by the Finance Controller.
International vendor payments require additional compliance clearance from Legal.

5. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any personal or financial relationship with vendors.
Failure to disclose conflicts of interest may result in disciplinary action.
The Ethics Committee reviews all conflict disclosures within 10 business days.

6. COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT
All procurement activities are subject to quarterly internal audit.
Non-compliance with this policy may result in purchase order cancellation.
Repeat violations will be escalated to Human Resources and senior leadership.
The Chief Compliance Officer publishes an annual procurement audit report.
